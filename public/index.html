What does Rentel cost me?

Browsing and requesting to rent is free. All fees are included in the room price — You ONLY pay what is listed, nothing else!

Are the photos real?

Rooms go through human review, and mislisted rooms can be reported in one tap for re-checking.

Can I filter by move-in date?

Yes — every room shows availability, and you can state your move-in date when you request to rent.
