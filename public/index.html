Questions renters ask

Everything else is on the About & safety page.

What does Roomy cost me?

Browsing and sending requests is free. A flat 10% service fee applies once you connect with a room — nothing before.

When do I get the owner's contact?

Only after the owner accepts your request. That protects both sides from spam and scam approaches.

Are the photos real?

Listings go through human review, and mislisted rooms can be reported in one tap for re-checking.

Can I filter by move-in date?

Yes — every listing shows availability, and you can state your move-in date when you send a request.

Own a room or a hostel?

List it in minutes. We handle the enquiries — you just say yes to the right tenant.
