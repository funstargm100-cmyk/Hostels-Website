Built for university students, so no one gets stranded on move-in day.

Rentel is a marketplace for hostels, self-contained rooms and shared houses near campuses across Ghana. It's built for university students hunting for a place to stay. We handle the whole rental for you: you request a room, we meet you to process it, you pay us what's listed, and we take care of the rest — you're never passed on to a landlord or agent to chase down yourself.

Verified rooms

You request the room, we meet you to process the rental, you pay us, and we complete it for you. You're never directed to an agent or landlord.

Privacy-jittered maps

Room locations are shown approximately for privacy — exact positions are shared once your rental is being processed.

No hidden fees

You only pay what's listed. No service charge, no booking fee, no surprise extras.

How renting works

  • Find a room you like and tap Rent this room — tell us your move-in date.
  • We review your request and meet you to process the rental. It's always Rentel you deal with — never an agent or landlord.
  • You pay us the amount listed on the room. That's the full price — there's nothing extra to pay.
  • We complete the rental and get you ready for move-in day.

Staying safe

  • Only ever pay Rentel — never pay cash to someone claiming to be a landlord or agent on the side.
  • If someone contacts you asking for money outside of Rentel, don't pay, and report it to us straight away.
  • Report a room immediately if a photo looks reused or the price seems too good to be true.
  • The price you see on a room is exactly what you pay — if anyone asks for more, tell us.

Terms, in plain language

By using Rentel, owners agree to list only rooms they're authorised to rent, and students agree to use room information for genuine rental requests only. Rentals are arranged and paid for through Rentel, and you only pay the listed price. The full legal terms and privacy policy are available on request from our support team.
