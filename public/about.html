About HostelHub

Ghana's trusted hostel and room rental marketplace.

Our Mission

HostelHub connects students and young professionals with verified room owners — without the stress of walking around or falling victim to scams. We act as the trusted middleman for every contact and transaction, ensuring both seekers and owners are protected.

🛡️ Safety Tips

✅
Always transact through HostelHub. Never pay a room owner directly before we confirm the connection.
✅
Inspect before you pay. We will arrange a viewing before any payment is made.
✅
Report suspicious listings. Use the report button on any listing that looks fake or contains contact info.
⚠️
Never share your payment details with anyone claiming to be a room owner outside the platform.
⚠️
Verified badge matters. Look for the green "Verified" badge — it means the owner has passed our KYC check.

💰 How Commission Works

HostelHub adds a transparent 10% platform fee to every listing price:

Owner's original priceGHS 1,000
Platform fee (10%)GHS 100
Seeker paysGHS 1,100

Of the 10%: 5% goes to the room owner/poster as a success fee, and 5% is retained by HostelHub to run the platform.

📄 Terms of Service

1. Contact Information Policy: Room owners must not include any phone numbers, email addresses, social media handles, or contact-soliciting phrases in their listings. Violations result in rejection, warnings, and potential account suspension.

2. Middleman Model: All connections between seekers and owners are facilitated exclusively by HostelHub. Direct contact outside the platform voids any platform protection.

3. KYC Requirement: Room owners must complete identity verification before their first listing goes live.

4. Listing Accuracy: Owners are responsible for the accuracy of their listings. Misleading listings will be removed.

5. Reviews: Reviews can only be submitted after a confirmed connection to prevent fake reviews.

6. Platform Rights: HostelHub reserves the right to remove any listing or suspend any account that violates these terms.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Data We Collect: Name, email, phone number, and KYC documents for account holders. Location data for listings (stored privately).

How We Use It: To facilitate room connections, verify identities, process payments, and send relevant notifications.

Location Privacy: Exact listing coordinates are never shown publicly. Seekers see only an approximate area (±150–300m offset). Exact address is shared only after a confirmed booking.

Contact Privacy: Owner contact details are never displayed to seekers. Seeker contact details are never displayed to owners until the platform manually connects them.

Data Security: All passwords are hashed. KYC documents are stored securely and accessible only to admins.
