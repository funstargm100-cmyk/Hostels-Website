Built so no one gets stranded on move-in day.

Roomy is a marketplace for hostels, self-contained rooms and shared houses across Ghana. We sit between seekers and owners so enquiries stay organised, contact details stay protected, and everyone knows what to expect before they commit.

Verified owners

Every host completes a light identity check before their first room goes live.

No direct contact upfront

We route requests through Roomy first, so numbers aren't shared until both sides are ready.

Privacy-jittered maps

Room locations are shown approximately until you've been in touch with the owner.

One transparent fee

A flat 10% service fee is split evenly between the platform and the owner — no surprise charges.

Staying safe

  • Always view a room (in person or on a video call) before sending any money.
  • Never pay a deposit outside of arrangements confirmed with the owner directly.
  • Report a room immediately if a photo looks reused or the price seems too good to be true.
  • Keep early conversations on Roomy so we can step in if something feels off.

Terms, in plain language

By using Roomy, owners agree to list only rooms they're authorised to rent, and seekers agree to use room information for genuine enquiries only. The full legal terms and privacy policy are available on request from our support team.
