About Roomy

Ghana's trusted hostel and room rental marketplace.

Our Mission

Roomy connects students and young professionals with verified room owners — without the stress of walking around or falling victim to scams. We act as the trusted middleman for every connection, ensuring both seekers and owners are protected.

Safety Tips

Always use Roomy to connect. Never deal with a room owner directly outside the platform.
Inspect before you commit. We will arrange a viewing before any agreement is made.
Report suspicious listings. Use the report button on any listing that looks fake or contains contact info.
Verified badge matters. Look for the green "Verified" badge on listings.

Terms of Service

1. Contact Information Policy: Room owners must not include any phone numbers, email addresses, social media handles, or contact-soliciting phrases in their listings. Violations result in rejection, warnings, and potential account suspension.

2. Middleman Model: All connections between seekers and owners are facilitated exclusively by Roomy. Direct contact outside the platform voids any platform protection.

3. Listing Accuracy: Owners are responsible for the accuracy of their listings. Misleading listings will be removed.

4. Reviews: Reviews can only be submitted after a confirmed connection to prevent fake reviews.

5. Platform Rights: Roomy reserves the right to remove any listing or suspend any account that violates these terms.

Privacy Policy

Data We Collect: Name, email, phone number for account holders. Location data for listings (stored privately).

How We Use It: To facilitate room connections, verify identities, and send relevant notifications.

Location Privacy: Exact listing coordinates are never shown publicly. Seekers see only an approximate area (±150–300m offset). Exact address is shared only after a confirmed connection.

Contact Privacy: Owner contact details are never displayed to seekers. Seeker contact details are never displayed to owners until the platform manually connects them.

Data Security: All passwords are hashed and stored securely.
